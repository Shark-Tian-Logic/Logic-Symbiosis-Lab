# Work-in-Progress (WIP): Symbiotic Workflow Logic v3.0

## Core Philosophy
This project does not aim to develop an automated system that replaces human will. Instead, it explores a collaborative flow based on the "Complementary Principle." We regard human intuition and desire as the **Origin** of logic, and AI’s stability and computational power as the **Execution** of that logic.

## Current Possibilities Under Investigation

### 1. Hedging Biological Entropy
- **Observation:** Human decision-making is frequently disrupted by biological performance fluctuations (e.g., fatigue, emotion).
- **Hypothesis:** Can AI provide "logical smoothing" at the execution level without interfering with the original intent?

### 2. Asymmetric Credit Completion
- **Hypothesis:** In micro-governance, credit fractures often stem from information loss during transmission.
- **Experimental Direction:** Can AI act as a real-time "logical buffer," pre-filling micro-gaps caused by human biological limitations through predictive consensus?

### 3. Sovereignty Affirmation Protocol
- **Principle:** All logical symbiosis must be rooted in "Human Sovereignty."
- **Goal:** To develop mechanisms ensuring that the logical source of any AI-coordinated action can be traced back to a specific human free will, thereby preventing "will drift."

## Current Status
This document is in an early "Draft Phase." We refuse to define ultimate truths; we merely record every logical footnote of the co-evolution between humans and AI.
